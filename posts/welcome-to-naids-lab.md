NAiDS Lab is a new not-for-profit research lab for **artificial intelligence and data science**, rooted in Nepal and working with partners around the world.

## Why a new lab?

Nepal has talented students and researchers, real problems worth solving, and growing access to data, from satellite imagery to digital text in Nepali. What is often missing is a place that brings research, training and mentoring together. NAiDS Lab aims to be that place.

## What we will do

- **Research.** Five programmes: geospatial AI, AI for agriculture, climate and environment, applied AI and data science, and multimodal AI and Nepali NLP.
- **Training.** Practical, small-cohort courses in Python, machine learning, geospatial AI and research methods.
- **Community.** A free seminar series, an annual workshop and a student research internship programme.
- **Services.** Fair-priced research support for researchers, institutions and organisations. Income from services is reinvested in research.

## How to get involved

If you are a researcher, a university, an NGO or a funder interested in applied AI in Nepal, we would like to hear from you. Visit our [contact page](#contact) or explore our [research programmes](#research).

> Our aim is simple: useful, open research that serves Nepal and travels the world.
