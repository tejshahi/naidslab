NAiDS Lab is a new not-for-profit, multidisciplinary research lab dedicated to advancing **data science and artificial intelligence for societal impact**. It is founded by **Dr Tej Bahadur Shahi**, is rooted in Nepal and works with partners around the world.

## Our vision: bridging data, algorithms and applications

We build intelligent models and analytical systems that extract insight from complex, multi-modal data, such as **satellite imagery**, **drone observations** and **socio-economic indicators**. Bringing these sources together sharpens environmental and agricultural monitoring, and reaches into smart cities, disaster management and sustainability planning.

Accuracy alone is not enough. A land manager, a conservation agency or a disaster planner needs to know *why* a model reached its answer before acting on it. Our central aim is **interpretable and explainable AI for remote sensing and Earth observation**: systems that give accurate predictions together with transparent, understandable insight for environmental conservation, resource management and climate change adaptation.

## Where this comes from

The Lab grows out of Dr Shahi's research journey. His PhD applied drones and AI to improve crop quality and quantity, and his work has applied machine learning across agriculture, health and finance. During his postdoctoral fellowship at QUT, he developed algorithms for the real-time analysis of satellite imagery to track grassland dynamics and vegetation health, working with interdisciplinary teams that bridge data science, AI and environmental science.

## Our research programmes

1. **Explainable GeoAI for Earth observation (flagship).** Interpretable models for satellite image time series, from farm to continental scale, including a foundational GeoAI model for Nepal's Sustainable Development Goals.
2. **Drones and AI for agriculture.** Detecting disease, weeds and water stress, and predicting yield, to improve crop quality and quantity.
3. **Multi-modal data intelligence for society.** Fusing satellite, drone and socio-economic data for smart cities, disaster management and sustainability planning, with explainable machine learning for health and finance.
4. **NLP for low-resource languages.** Open datasets and efficient models for Nepali, code-mixed Roman Nepali and other languages of Nepal.

## How we will create impact

- **Research funding.** We will seek national and international research grants, alone and with partners.
- **Dissemination.** Peer-reviewed publications, talks at leading forums such as IEEE IGARSS and IEEE ICDM, and policy briefings. Our [publications](#publications) list already holds 40 papers.
- **A collaborative team.** Research students, postdoctoral researchers and faculty members working together on shared projects.
- **Training and community.** Courses, a free seminar series and student research internships.
